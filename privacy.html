Hallway Dash: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Hallway Dash ("the game") is made by the Hallway Dash developer ("we"). Contact: ibweintraub@gmail.com.

Summary

  • In the Android app we ask your age with a neutral slider and keep only your age group (under 13, 13–15, 16+). We never store your exact age.
  • On the website there is no age question. Every browser player gets the safe 13–15 settings: no analytics, non-personalized ads, and ads only appear when you choose to watch one (for a revive or a reward).
  • Players under 13: we do not collect personal information. No analytics, no online leaderboard, no friends, no cloud sign-in, and ads are child-directed and non-personalized.
  • You can delete all your data at any time: Settings → Privacy → Delete all my data.

    We don't sell personal information. We don't ask for your real name, email, location, contacts, camera, photos or microphone. Nicknames are shown on leaderboards: please don't use your real name. School boards only show the school's name, never a town, address or class. New school names are checked by a person before anyone else can see them. Nicknames and school names go through a word filter, and anyone can report or block a player.

    Ads

    Ads are rated G. Under 13: ads are flagged as child-directed (COPPA) and non-personalized. 13–15: non-personalized. 16+: personalized only if you agree in Google's consent form (shown where the law requires it); you can change your choice in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices. Google's ad policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

    Third-party services

    Google AdMob, Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Analytics), Google Play Billing and Google Play Games Services. Their privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

    Your choices and rights

    Switch off analytics or personalized ads in Settings → Privacy (16+). Delete everything with Delete all my data. You can also email us at ibweintraub@gmail.com to access or delete your data. If you're in the EU/UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA/CPRA) you have the right to access, correct and delete your data and to opt out of "sharing" for ads.

    How to delete your data

    You can delete your data at any time, without deleting the app:

    1. In the game: Settings → Privacy → Delete all my data → Delete everything. This wipes your progress and settings on this device, your online leaderboard entry, and your cloud save.

    2. By email: send a message to ibweintraub@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Hallway Dash data". Include your leaderboard nickname or 6-letter friend code if you have one, so we can find your online entry. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

    What gets deleted: game progress, nickname, scores, school, school requests, friend code, anonymous player id and cloud save. Purchase records are kept by Google Play as required for payments and refunds. Advertising data is held by Google; you can reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google → Ads).

    Children

    If you believe a child under 13 gave us personal information, email ibweintraub@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

    Changes

    We'll update this page and the date above if anything changes.
