Power-ups, Runners & Sidekicks

Hall Passes, Paper Planes, Juice Boxes, Heel Wheels and the runners and pets that make them better.

Power-ups float above the hallway during runs. Grab one and a timer appears at the side of the screen. They're the difference between a decent run and a record, so it's worth knowing exactly what each one does and how to make it last longer.

Power-ups you find in runs

🎫

Hall Pass

Makes you invincible: smash straight through people, carts and signs without stopping. Starts at 6 seconds and gets longer with upgrades.

✈️

Paper Plane

Lifts you into the air and flies you over the whole hallway, collecting coins along the way. There are 12 plane designs to unlock.

🧃

Juice Box

A burst of speed plus a coin magnet that pulls coins in from nearby. Great for padding your cash total.

👟

Super Sneakers

Green spring shoes that make your jumps much higher. High enough to clear people, so you can jump over blockers too.

Tip: when a power-up is about to run out, the timer at the side of the screen flashes. Use those last seconds to get into a safe lane, not to grab one more coin.

Other things to collect

  • Coins and dollar bills add to your cash. The Piggy Bank upgrade and some runners add a bonus percentage.
  • Gold Stars ★ are rare and valuable. Spend them on revives, premium runners and sidekicks.
  • Heel Wheels pickups give you a free pair of Heel Wheels.
  • Outfit Tokens buy outfits for your runner (Varsity, Neon Night, Pastel, Midnight and Golden).
  • Letter tiles spell the Daily Word. Complete it for Gold Stars and cash.
  • Souvenirs appear on field trips: fossils at the museum, seashells at the aquarium, gears at the science center and tickets at the arcade. Collect enough and you unlock that trip's runner.
  • Event tokens like candy corn, snowflakes or pizza slices appear during limited-time events.

One-run boosts

Boosts are used up when you start a run with them. They're best saved for when you're chasing a specific record or a tough campaign star.

  • Heel Wheels: double-tap during a run to skate faster, do tricks on jumps and survive one crash.
  • Head Start: start with a 500 m invincible sprint.
  • Score Doubler and Coin Doubler: double score or double coins for one run.
  • Hall Pass Start: start a run with 10 seconds of invincibility.
  • Football Pads: a 12-second Hall Pass the moment you start.

Sidekicks

A sidekick is a pet that runs next to you and helps out:

  • Husky Mascot: pulls in coins from the next lane and fetches you a power-up every 25 seconds.
  • Shiba Inu: +25% Gold Stars, and barks a warning when something is about to hit you.
  • Fox (School Year Pass reward): +10% score, and once per run leaps in to save you from a crash.
  • Golden Pup: +10% score, and starts every run by bringing you a Juice Box.
  • Arctic Fox: +25% Gold Stars, and calms chasers so chases end 3 seconds sooner.

Cosmetics

The rest of the Locker Room is pure style: hats (from baseball caps and beanies to a graduation cap), backpacks, Heel Wheels designs, shoes, paper plane designs, and even your hallway's lockers, banners, floors and lights. Cosmetics don't change how the game plays, so buy whatever you like the look of.

What to buy first

  1. Hall Pass upgrades. Longer invincibility saves more runs than anything else.
  2. Track Star or another runner whose perk fits how you play. Faster lane switching makes high speeds much more manageable.
  3. Push Power. A faster push means more escapes from triple-blocked rows.
  4. Piggy Bank, once you're doing longer runs, so every run pays for the next upgrade faster.
  5. Save Gold Stars for revives during runs that are heading for a personal best.
