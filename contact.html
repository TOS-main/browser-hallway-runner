Contact

Questions, bug reports, ideas and privacy requests all go to one inbox, read by the developer.

Hallway Dash is made by one developer, and every message is read by that same person. The best way to reach us is email: ibweintraub@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

What to include

🐞 Bug reports

Tell us what you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead. It helps a lot to include your device (for example "school Chromebook" or "iPhone 13"), your browser, and a screenshot if you can. If it happened during a run, which school or mode you were playing.

💡 Ideas and feedback

New obstacles, runners, field trips, modes: we'd love to hear them. Many features in the game, like the simpler home screen and the Level Maker, came from player suggestions.

🏫 Schools on the leaderboard

If your school is missing, ask for it from the leaderboard inside the game; a moderator checks every new school. If a school name is wrong or a player is breaking the rules, use the 🚩 Report button next to it in the game, or email us.

🔒 Privacy and data deletion

Use the subject line "Delete my Hallway Dash data" and include your leaderboard username or 6-letter friend code if you have one. We delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm. You can also delete everything yourself in the game: Settings → Privacy → Delete all my data. See the privacy policy.

💳 Purchases

Include the time of purchase, what you bought and the receipt email from Stripe. We'll make sure you get what you paid for or arrange a refund.

👩‍🏫 Teachers and parents

Questions about using Hallway Dash with students are welcome. Start with our page for parents and teachers.

Please don't send us personal information we don't need, and if you're under 13, ask a parent or guardian to email for you.

Before writing, you might find your answer in the FAQ.
