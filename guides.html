Where should I start?

Brand new? Play the in-game tutorial, then read How to Play. It takes five minutes and covers everything the tutorial doesn't.

Keep crashing at the same point? The Obstacle Guide and tips 1 to 10 in Tips & Strategy are about reacting faster and earlier.

Stuck on a campaign stage? Check the stage goal in the Campaign guide, and bring a Heel Wheels pair or a shield runner to boss stages.

Chasing a high score? Finish missions to raise your multiplier, then read about difficulty and speed multipliers in Game Modes.
