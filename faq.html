Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about playing, saving, ads, safety and getting help.

Playing

Is Hallway Dash free?

Yes. The whole game, including every school, the campaign, all game modes and the Level Maker, is free to play. Ads help pay for it. Players 13 and up can optionally buy a few items in the School Store, like No Ads or a Starter Pack, but nothing is needed to finish the game.

Do I need to download anything or make an account?

No. Hallway Dash runs in your web browser. Just open hallwaydash.org/play/ and tap to play. There's no account to create. Signing in with Google is optional and only used to carry your save to another device.

Which devices does it work on?

Any modern browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on Windows, Mac, Chromebooks, Android phones and tablets, iPhones and iPads. On slower devices the game picks lower graphics automatically, and you can change it under Settings → Graphics quality.

Is there an app?

An Android version is coming to Google Play. In the meantime, the website works well on phones. Add it to your home screen from your browser's menu to open it like an app.

Does it work offline?

Once the game has loaded, single-player runs keep working if your connection drops. Online features such as leaderboards, duels and shared levels need a connection.

What are the controls?

Swipe left, right, up or down on a touch screen. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys or WASD, plus Q to push, E for Heel Wheels and X for the combo finisher. Press C in the game to see every control, or read how to play.

Why did my run end when I only touched someone?

Running into a person, a cart or a class in line head-on ends a run. Clipping something from the side only starts a 10-second chase, but if you hit anything else during that chase, you're caught. The obstacle guide explains which obstacles need a jump, a slide or a lane change.

The game is too hard / too easy. Can I change it?

Yes. Pick Easy, Normal, Hard or Insane, and separately a speed from Chill to Turbo, in Settings or on the Modes screen. Harder settings score more. See difficulty and speed.

Saving and progress

Does my progress save?

Yes, automatically, after everything you do, in the browser you're playing in. Come back on the same browser and everything is there.

How do I move my progress to another computer or phone?

Two ways. Sign in with Google from the "Saved" button on the home screen and your progress syncs to your account after every run (13 and up). Or open Settings, copy your save code, and paste it into the game on the other device.

I cleared my browser data and lost my progress. Can you restore it?

If you were signed in with Google or saved a save code, yes: sign in again or paste the code. Otherwise, progress lives only in your browser, so we can't restore it. That's why we recommend signing in or keeping a save code.

Do the old links to the game still work?

Yes. The game moved to /play/ in November 2026 so the site could have guides and help pages, but your save is unaffected and old links still open the game or the home page.

Friends and leaderboards

How do I play against a friend?

Finish a run and tap Copy duel code, then send it to your friend. When they paste it into Duel a Friend, they run the exact same hallway with your score showing next to theirs. You can also both start the same hallway at the same moment with Race now. See duels.

How do I add my school?

Open the leaderboard and choose your school from the list, or ask for it to be added. New school names are checked by a moderator before anyone else sees them. Your runs then count toward your school's score.

Someone has a rude name on the leaderboard. What do I do?

Tap the 🚩 next to their name and pick a reason. You can also block them so they disappear from your boards. Reports go only to the moderator, who never sees your name.

Why can't I see the leaderboards?

Online features are turned off for players who told the age check they're under 13, to protect their privacy. Everything else in the game still works.

Ads, purchases and safety

Where do ads appear?

Only when you choose to watch one for a reward, and in a short break between some runs for players 13 and up. Never during a run, and on the website never for players under 13. Any purchase turns off the ads between runs.

Is it safe for kids?

We built it to be. On the website, under-13 players get no ads, no online features and no data collection beyond an anonymous daily play count. There is no chat. Read the full explanation for parents and teachers.

I bought something and didn't get it.

Purchases are confirmed with the payment provider before they're added, which can take a few seconds. If it still hasn't appeared, contact us with the time of purchase and your receipt, and we'll sort it out.

How do I delete my data?

In the game, open Settings → Privacy → Delete all my data. Or email us with the subject "Delete my Hallway Dash data". Details are in the privacy policy.

Other

Who makes Hallway Dash?

An independent student developer. Read more about the game.

I found a bug or have an idea.

Please tell us! Email the details (what device and browser, and what happened) using the contact page. Lots of features started as player suggestions.

Is this the same as other runner games?

Hallway Dash is its own original game. It shares the three-lane runner format that many games use, but its school setting, characters, obstacles, music and levels were all made for it (with free public-domain 3D models and sounds, credited on the about page).

Ready to run? Hallway Dash is free and plays right in your browser. No download or account needed.
