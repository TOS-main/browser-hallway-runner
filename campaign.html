Campaign & Boss Guide

From elementary school to college and beyond: how the stages work, how to earn every star, and how to escape each boss.

The Campaign turns Hallway Dash's endless hallway into a journey: 100 stages across five schools, plus 50 more on four field trips. Each stage ends at a classroom door, has a bell timer, and asks you to do something specific for the third star. Every tenth stage is a boss chase.

How a stage works

You start at one end of the hallway and your destination, like Art Class, the Science Lab or Lecture Hall 3B, is at the other. A bell timer counts down at the top of the screen. The last ten seconds tick out loud. Reach the door before the bell and you pass; the next stage unlocks.

Within each school, stages get longer, faster and busier as you go. The first stage of Elementary School is a gentle walk to Art Class; the last stage of the College Campus is a sprint to Commencement.

Earning three stars

  • ★ One star: beat the bell.
  • ★★ Two stars: finish quickly, with plenty of time left on the clock.
  • ★★★ Three stars: complete the stage's goal.

Stage goals rotate between five types, shown before you start:

  • Grab cash: collect a set amount of money on the way.
  • No bumps: finish without starting a single chase. The hardest goal for most players.
  • Power-ups: grab a number of power-ups.
  • Jumps: jump over a number of obstacles.
  • Dodges: dodge past a number of people.
Tip: you don't have to get three stars the first time. Pass the stage, keep going, and come back with better upgrades. Stars add up, and you can see your total on the map.

The five schools

1. Elementary School (Easy)

Bright orange floors, sky-blue walls and wide lanes. You'll meet classes walking in line, little kids, backpacks, cones, trash cans, lunch trays and the odd science-fair volcano. Destinations include the Music Room, the Library, Recess, Picture Day, the Book Fair and finally Graduation. This is where you learn the basics.

2. Middle School (Medium)

Teal floors and orange walls. Lanes get a little narrower and the hallway fills up with walking pairs, kids at their lockers, rushers sprinting the other way, teachers, mop buckets, painter's ladders and the robotics club robot. You'll run to Band Practice, a Math Quiz, the Dance, the Science Fair and even Detention (yikes), ending at the 8th Grade Send-off.

3. High School (Hard)

Blue floors and red lockers, with the busiest hallways in the game: more teachers and hall monitors, AV carts, cheer pyramids and marching band drummers. Destinations include Chemistry Lab, AP Calculus, the Pep Rally, Driver's Ed, Homecoming, Model UN and Graduation.

4. College Campus (Expert)

Outdoors on the quad, under open sky. Strict professors, sleep-deprived students and runaway e-scooters crossing your path. Some stages are Rainy Days with puddles and umbrellas. You'll run to Office Hours, Midterms, the Career Fair, Finals Week, a Thesis Defense and Commencement.

5. After-School Athletics (Bonus)

The gym and sports fields: basketballs, sprinklers, hurdles, the coach and cheer pyramids. Run to Basketball Practice, Swim Team, Dodgeball, the Championship and the Trophy Ceremony.

From Middle School onward, some stages are Night School stages with the lights turned down, so you'll need to read obstacles earlier.

Field trip stages

After the schools come four field trips, each with 12 or 13 stages and their own look:

  • Museum Field Trip: marble halls, velvet ropes, Ancient Egypt, the Gem Room and the T-Rex.
  • Aquarium Field Trip: blue tunnels, the Penguins, the Jellyfish Room and the Shark Tunnel.
  • Science Center Trip: the Robot Lab, the Tornado, the Planetarium and the Mars Rover.
  • Arcade Day Out (the final chapter): neon carpet, the Claw Machine, Skee-Ball, Pinball Row and the Prize Counter.

Field trips are also a rotating Infinite Run mode: a different trip runs for three weeks at a time, with souvenirs to collect and a runner to unlock. See game modes.

Boss stages

Every tenth stage, and the last stage of each chapter, is a boss stage. The boss starts chasing you shortly after the start and never gives up. Any hit means you're caught, including side bumps that would normally only start a chase.

👔

Principal Showdown

"PRINCIPAL'S ORDERS: STOP RUNNING!" The Principal is the boss in Elementary School, High School, the gym chapter and some field trips.

🛴

Scooter Patrol

"HALL PASS. NOW." A hall monitor on a scooter, the boss in Middle School, College and some field trips.

💪

Bully Chase

"YOU'RE GOING DOWN!" The final stage of each chapter is a chase by the school bully.

How to beat a boss stage

  1. Play clean, not fast. Coins don't matter here. Skip risky coin lines.
  2. Stay in the middle lane as much as possible so you always have two ways out.
  3. Use your push the moment all three lanes look blocked. Don't save it.
  4. Bring a shield: Heel Wheels, Class President's shield, the Fox sidekick or a Hall Pass Start boost can each absorb one mistake.
  5. Watch the timer, not the boss. Bosses look scary but they can only catch you if you hit something.
