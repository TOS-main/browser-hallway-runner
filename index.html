What is Hallway Dash?

Hallway Dash takes the classic three-lane endless runner and drops it into the place everyone remembers: the school hallway between classes. Instead of trains and barriers, you dodge the things that actually block you on the way to class, like a class walking in line, a student digging in their locker, a teacher stepping out yelling "NO RUNNING!", or a janitor's mop bucket in the middle of the floor.

Every run is different. The hallway is built as you go, mixing groups of students, wet-floor signs, swinging classroom doors, lunch tables you can run along the top of, and classroom shortcuts full of coins. The longer you last, the faster it gets. Bump into something from the side and it chases you for ten seconds; hit anything else while it's on your heels and you're caught.

Between runs you spend the coins you collected on new runners, outfits, hats, Heel Wheels designs, backpacks, sidekick pets and upgrades that make your power-ups last longer. There's a 150-stage campaign from elementary school to college and on to museum, aquarium, science-center and arcade field trips, with a boss chase every tenth stage.

A quick look

How to play in 30 seconds

On a phone or tablet, swipe left or right to switch lanes, swipe up to jump and swipe down to slide. On a computer, use the ← → arrow keys (or A D) to switch lanes, ↑ or Space to jump and ↓ to slide. Low things like trash cans and backpacks get jumped. High things like banners and posters get slid under. People, carts and classes in line block the whole lane, so go around them.

The first time you play, a short tutorial walks you through each move with real obstacles. After that it's you, the hallway and the bell. Our full how-to-play guide covers pushing, Heel Wheels, combos and chases in detail.
