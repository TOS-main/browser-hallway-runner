Updates & Developer Notes

What changed in Hallway Dash and why, written by the developer. Newest first.

Hallway Dash gets updated often, sometimes several times a day. These notes collect the bigger changes, explain why they were made, and are written by the developer. Newest first.

November 25, 2026

A real home for Hallway Dash

Until today, hallwaydash.org opened straight into the game. That was fast, but it meant there was nowhere to explain how the game works, to answer common questions, or to tell parents and teachers how we handle safety. So the site now has a proper home page, and the game itself lives at hallwaydash.org/play/.

Along with it come in-depth guides: how to play, a full obstacle guide, power-ups and runners, the campaign and bosses, every game mode, 25 tips, a FAQ and a page for parents and teachers. Your progress is not affected: saves stay in your browser exactly as before, and old links to the game still work.

November 11, 2026

Card checkout, a new shop and cartoon runners

The School Store on the website can now take card payments through Stripe Checkout for a small set of optional items: a one-time Starter Pack, permanent No Ads and Coin Doubler upgrades, the season's Gold Track, and cash and Gold Star packs. Every item has fixed contents (nothing is random), any purchase also turns off the short ads between runs, and purchases are checked on our server before anything is granted. Players under 13 never see the store.

This update also added four chunky cartoon runners (Pixie, Bolt, Shades and Captain Recess), made hitboxes match the true size of each obstacle so near-misses feel fair, made the game start faster, and picks smarter graphics settings automatically on low-end Chromebooks and older phones.

November 9, 2026

The Level Maker

The biggest feature since launch: build your own hallway. Place obstacles lane by lane, set the speed and difficulty, turn the lights out, test it, and share it. To keep shared levels fair, you have to beat a level yourself before you can share it. Players can browse Featured, Newest, Most liked, Most played and Hardest levels, like the ones they enjoy, and report anything inappropriate to the moderator.

The same week brought unique usernames (changeable once a month), one anonymous player id per browser so your records stay yours, smoother lane changes, a skippable tutorial, a mute button, and lots of fixes. We also added anonymous daily play counts so we can see how many people play without collecting anything about who they are.

November 8, 2026

Simpler home screen, automatic saving

Testers told us the home screen had too many buttons. Now it's one big Tap to Play and four big buttons (Locker, Modes, Ranks and More), and everything else lives on the More screen, unlocking gradually as you play. Your progress now saves by itself after everything you do, and you can sign in with Google to carry it to another browser, or copy an encrypted save code as a backup.

We also made controls snappier, brightened the hallway, added new animations and made "add your school" instant. Analytics and ads now wait until after the age check, so nothing loads before we know how old a player is.

November 7, 2026

Moving to hallwaydash.org

Hallway Dash moved to its own address, hallwaydash.org. Old links from the original GitHub address forward automatically. We also published the Terms of Service, and added your school to the intro, with a school record to set or break.

November 6, 2026

School leaderboards and the theme song

You can now add your school and see how it ranks against other schools. Because school boards are seen by students, they come with rules (usernames only, no personal information), a report and block button next to every name, and a moderator who checks new schools and reports. Leaderboards are now split by difficulty and speed, so every board is a fair fight.

Hallway Dash also got its own original theme song, "Hallway Rush", with an intro that plays when a run starts and optional lyrics on the start screen. Other fixes: track hurdles are now bold red and white so they're easy to see, characters no longer sink into the floor, and teachers step into lanes more fairly.

November 5, 2026

Head hops, duels and the side mode list

A huge day. Head hops now work on every normal-height person, with lines of students to hop across. The school store, locker boxes, cafeteria and schoolyard zones, a new HUD and friendlier faces arrived. Super Sneakers let you jump over people when you're high enough. On computers, all the play modes now sit down the side of the menu with keyboard shortcuts, and Duel a Friend lets two people race the same hallway.

We connected the world leaderboard and friends features, and set up ads so that players under 13 never get ad requests at all. The privacy policy gained clear steps for deleting your data.

November 4, 2026

Hallway Dash comes to the browser

The first browser version of Hallway Dash went live: the whole 3D game in a single page that runs on phones, tablets and computers, and keeps working offline once loaded. The same day brought a tutorial you can skip, new obstacles, bleachers to run on, Heel Wheels designs, runner abilities, lunch-table platforms, keyboard controls for computers, save codes and friend challenges, and Ms. Moore, the little teacher.

What's next

  • The Android version on Google Play, with the same hallways and runners.
  • More field trip locations and more events through the school year.
  • More guides on this site as new features arrive.

Have an idea or found a bug? Tell us. Many of the changes above started as player suggestions.

Ready to run? Hallway Dash is free and plays right in your browser. No download or account needed.
