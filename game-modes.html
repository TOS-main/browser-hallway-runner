Game Modes

Hallway Dash is more than one endless hallway. Here's every way to play, and how difficulty and speed change your score.

On a computer, all of these modes are listed down the side of the home screen; on a phone, tap Modes. Here's what each one is, who it's best for, and how it scores.

🏃 Infinite Run

The classic mode. The hallway never ends and keeps speeding up. The hallway's look cycles through the schools as you go, and every so often a hunter (the Principal, a hall monitor or the bully) comes after you. Your score is saved to the leaderboards for your difficulty and speed, so an Easy score never competes with an Insane one. Press Enter or tap anywhere on the home screen to start.

Best for: high scores, missions and earning coins.

🎓 Campaign

150 stages with a finish line and a bell timer: five schools from elementary to college plus four field trips. Each stage has a goal for the third star, and every tenth stage is a boss. Read the full campaign and boss guide.

Best for: learning the game step by step, and steady rewards.

⚔️ Duel a Friend and Race Now

Duels let two people run the exact same hallway. Finish a run, tap Copy duel code and send the code to a friend. When they paste it in, they get your hallway, and your score sits next to theirs on the side of the screen so they can see who's winning. Codes work between phones and browsers.

Race now is the live version: you and a friend both start the same hallway at the same moment after a 30-second countdown, then compare scores at the end. You can also race a friend's best run as a see-through "ghost" runner in the next lane.

Best for: settling arguments at lunch.

📅 Daily Challenge

One new challenge every day: a set hallway style, sometimes with a twist (Lights Out or Hardcore rules), and a target score. Beat it for three stars. Everyone gets the same challenge on the same day.

🦴 Field Trips

A field trip location runs for three weeks at a time, rotating between the Museum, the Aquarium, the Science Center and the Arcade. During a trip, runs take place in that location and you collect its souvenir (fossils, seashells, gears or tickets). Souvenirs fill a reward track worth cash, Gold Stars and Outfit Tokens, and the last reward is a runner you can only get on that trip.

🏆 Weekly Tournament

Everyone in the world runs the same hallway for a week. The board shows the best scores, and the top three at the end of the week win Gold Stars. Because the hallway is identical for everyone, it's the purest test of skill in the game.

🛠️ Level Maker

Build your own hallway. Pick the setting and speed, place obstacles lane by lane, choose how hard it should be, and turn on Lights out if you're feeling mean. Use Test to play it right away, and practice mode with checkpoints to learn tricky sections.

To share a level you have to beat it yourself first, so every shared level is possible. Other players can browse levels by Featured, Newest, Most liked, Most played and Hardest, like them, and report anything inappropriate. Each level has an ID you can copy and send to friends.

Events, League and Clubs

  • Events run for a limited time and change the hallway: Halloween Hallway, Snow Day, Fire Drill Week, Hall Pass Rush, Cafeteria Food Fight and Prom Night. Each has its own token to collect, a special obstacle, and an exclusive runner.
  • League: run for points to climb the league tiers with other players.
  • School Year Pass: rewards for every tier you reach just by playing, including the Fox sidekick at tier 15.
  • Missions: three at a time. Finish all three to raise your score multiplier and win a Mystery Box.
  • Trophies: lifetime goals like total distance run, each with several tiers of rewards.

Difficulty and speed

Difficulty and speed are separate settings, and both change your score.

DifficultyWhat changesScore
EasySlower start, fewer obstacles, more space between rowsx1
NormalThe standard gamex1
HardFaster start, more obstacles, higher top speedx1.5
InsaneFastest start, the most obstacles, the highest top speedx2

However hard it gets, there is always at least one open lane. Speed is set separately: Chill (x0.8 score), Normal, Fast (x1.25) and Turbo (x1.6). It works in every mode. Tournament and duel runs use fixed settings so they're fair.
