About Hallway Dash

Hallway Dash is a free 3D endless runner set in a school hallway. You dodge crowds of students, teachers, janitor carts and hall monitors on your way to class, collect coins, unlock outfits and pets, and race through more than 100 stages from elementary school to college.

It runs in any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with no download or account needed. An Android version is coming to Google Play.

Who makes it

Hallway Dash is made by an independent student developer. The characters, music and sounds are free CC0 and public-domain assets, credited in the game files.

Safe for every age

The game asks for a player's age group the first time it opens. Players under 13 get a kid-safe version: no personal information is collected, online features are off, and no ad requests are sent. Players 13 to 15 only see non-personalized ads. Read the full Privacy Policy for details.

Ads

The free game is supported by ads from Google AdSense. Ads appear only in two places: when a player chooses to watch an ad for a reward, and in a short break between some runs.

Visit counts
