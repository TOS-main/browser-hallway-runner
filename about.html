About Hallway Dash

Who makes the game, how it's built, and what we care about.

Hallway Dash is a free 3D endless runner set in school hallways. You dodge crowds of students, teachers, janitor carts and hall monitors on your way to class, collect coins, unlock runners, outfits and pets, and race through 150 stages from elementary school to college.

Who makes it

Hallway Dash is made by an independent student developer. It started from a simple question every student has asked while weaving through a packed hallway between classes: what if this were a game? The first browser version went live in November 2026, and it's been updated almost every day since, often based on what players asked for.

What we care about

  • Fair fun. Every hallway always has an open lane, hitboxes match what you see, and the game is completely playable without paying.
  • Safe for students. Players under 13 get a version with no ads, no online features and no personal data collected. No real names anywhere. Leaderboards are moderated. Read more for parents and teachers.
  • Works everywhere. The whole game runs in one web page, on school Chromebooks, old laptops and phones, with no install.
  • Honest ads. Ads only appear when you choose to watch one, or in a short break between some runs for players 13 and up. Never in the middle of a run.

How it's made

Hallway Dash is built with web technology: the 3D graphics use the open-source three.js library, and the entire game, including models, sounds and fonts, is packed into one file so it loads once and keeps working offline. Leaderboards, friends and shared levels use Google Firebase. The original theme song, "Hallway Rush", was made for the game.

The 3D characters, animations and pets come from generous artists who release their work into the public domain (CC0):

  • Characters and animals by Quaternius
  • Cartoon runners and animations by Kay Lousberg (KayKit)
  • Announcer voice lines by Kenney
  • Extra music by Kevin MacLeod via FreePD (public domain)
  • Fonts: Luckiest Guy and Patrick Hand (SIL Open Font License)

The game in numbers

  • 5 schools and 4 field-trip locations
  • 150 campaign stages, with a boss every tenth stage
  • 40+ obstacles, from wet-floor signs to cheer pyramids
  • 30+ runners, 5 sidekicks and hundreds of cosmetic combinations
  • 11 ways to play, plus the Level Maker

What's next

An Android version is on its way to Google Play. New field trips and events arrive through the school year. You can follow along on the updates page.

Ads and visit counts on this website

The free game is supported by ads from Google AdSense. This site uses Google Analytics to count visits from people who told the game they're 16 or older: which page was opened, the site that linked to it, and rough location, device and browser type. It sets no cookies, keeps no visitor id, and has Google signals and ad features turned off, so no visit is tied to a person.
