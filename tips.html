Tips & Strategy

25 practical tips for longer runs and bigger scores, from your very first run to the Insane difficulty.

These tips come from thousands of test runs while building the game. They're grouped from beginner to expert, so start at the top and work down. If you're brand new, read how to play first.

Reading the hallway

1. Look halfway down the hall, not at your runner

Your runner is always in the same spot on screen. Everything you need to react to is coming from further away. Train your eyes to sit about halfway up the screen and you'll see rows of obstacles a full second earlier.

2. Sort, then act

Every obstacle is low (jump), high (slide) or a blocker (switch lanes). Don't try to remember forty obstacles; just ask "floor, head or person?" The obstacle guide has the full list.

3. Live in the middle lane

From the middle lane you can escape left or right. From an outside lane you only have one way out, and lockers popping open and kids at their lockers only appear on the sides. Drift back to the middle whenever the hallway is quiet.

4. Coins mark a safe line

Coin trails are placed where a runner can actually go. When you're unsure which lane is open, a line of coins is a good bet.

5. There's always an open lane

Even on Insane, the game never builds a row with all three lanes completely blocked. If it looks that way, look again: one of them is a jump or a slide, not a wall.

Movement and timing

6. Jump a little earlier as you speed up

The faster you run, the more ground you cover while you're in the air. At the start of a run jump late; after a few thousand metres, jump earlier than feels natural.

7. Fix a bad jump with a slide

Jumped too early into a banner? Swipe down in mid-air. You drop to the floor instantly and go straight into a slide.

8. Buffer your next jump

Swipe up just before you land and you'll jump again the moment your feet touch the floor. This is how you clear rows of hurdles in rhythm.

9. Commit to lane changes

Changing your mind halfway through a lane switch is how most side bumps happen. Decide, swipe once, and trust it.

10. Don't swipe into the walls

Swiping toward the lockers from an outside lane slams them, and the locker's owner chases you. On a phone, it's easy to over-swipe when you're nervous.

Chases and survival

11. A chase is a warning, not a game over

Bumping something from the side gives you ten seconds of pressure. Slow down mentally: skip coins, stay in safe lanes and just survive. Ten clean seconds and they give up.

12. Use push as a panic button

Push recharges, so there's no reason to hoard it. The moment a row looks impossible, push. Upgrade Push Power early so it's ready more often.

13. Save Heel Wheels for the hard part

Heel Wheels survive one crash. They're worth more at 3,000 metres, when everything is fast, than in the first ten seconds.

14. Watch for hunters

When the warning appears that a hunter is after you, keep out of the red lane they're running in until they pass.

15. Use the revive wisely

Gold Stars are rare. Spend them to revive on runs that are close to your best, not on runs that ended at 200 m.

Scoring big

16. Finish your missions

Every completed set of three missions raises your score multiplier, up to x30. That multiplier applies to every future run, so it's the biggest long-term score boost in the game.

17. Chain head hops

Each extra head in a chain is worth more than the last. Lines of students waiting for the water fountain are spaced perfectly for hopping. Five in a row is a "Crowd Surfer".

18. Build to x3 and fire the finisher

Close calls, dodges, hops and tricks build your combo. At x3, the finisher clears every obstacle on screen and gives a big bonus. Fire it when the hallway ahead is crowded, so you clear more.

19. Take the shortcuts

Yellow "SHORTCUT" classroom doors give you a stretch of free coins with nothing to dodge. You can't switch lanes inside, so make sure the exit is clear.

20. Answer the pop quiz

A correct answer is worth bonus points, coins and a combo boost. The answers are simple math, so read the question as soon as it appears.

21. Pick your difficulty on purpose

Hard scores x1.5 and Insane x2, and Fast and Turbo speeds add more. A good Hard run often beats a great Normal run, but a short Insane run beats nothing. Find the hardest setting where you can still last a couple of minutes.

Progress and spending

22. Upgrade the Hall Pass first

Longer invincibility both saves runs and lets you smash through crowds for points. It's the best early purchase.

23. Choose a runner for your weak spot

Struggling at high speed? Track Star switches lanes 20% faster. Always getting caught in chases? Space Club makes them last only 7 seconds. Want more stars? Science Fair Champ. See every runner's perk.

24. Come back every day

The Daily Login Reward, the free gift, the Daily Spin, the Mystery Locker and the Daily Word all reset daily. Even one quick run a day adds up fast.

25. Take breaks

Endless runners reward focus. If you've crashed early five times in a row, take a break. You'll almost always do better after a few minutes away.
